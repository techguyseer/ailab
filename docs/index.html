The Process

01. Collection

Recovery of post-consumer plastic waste from coastal ecosystems.

MATERIAL: HDPE / PET | SOURCE: COASTAL WATERS

02. Processing

Mechanical shredding and thermal purification into raw pellets.

TEMP: 260°C | PROCESS: POLYMERIZATION

03. Weaving

Precision extrusion into high-tensile recycled yarn.

DENSITY: 450 GSM | WEAVE: RIPSTOP

04. Assembly

Ergonomic construction combining recycled uppers and soles.

BOND: WATER-BASED ADHESIVE | WEIGHT: 240g
